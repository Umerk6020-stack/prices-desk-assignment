# AI Verification and Reflection

## AI-Suggested Questions
1. How have food prices changed over time?
2. Have rising prices caused poverty?
3. Are food prices rising faster than wages?
4. Which products increased most in price?
5. How do prices compare between products?

## Rejected Questions

1. Poverty: Price data alone cannot prove
   that rising prices caused poverty.

2. Wages: Without wage data, we cannot
   compare wage growth with price changes.

## Verification
I checked the dataset variables to see
which questions could be answered.
Questions requiring unavailable data
were rejected.

## Reflection
Price data can show how the prices of
different products change over time.
It can help identify which items have
experienced the largest increases.
However, price changes alone cannot
explain why prices increased or whether
people can afford these products.
Affordability depends on income and
household spending, which may not be
included in the dataset. Similarly,
price data cannot establish whether
inflation caused poverty or unemployment.
Therefore, conclusions must be based
on the variables actually available.
AI can help generate research questions,
but its suggestions should be checked
carefully before being accepted.
