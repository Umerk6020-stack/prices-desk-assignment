Me: Staple A's mean was 158.71 with the price 1250. Without it, the mean was 111.26.

Me: The median stayed at 112.50 in both cases. It was less affected by the unusual price.

Classmate: So when should we use the mean?

Me: When the data has no major outliers, the mean can be useful.
